### Hey there 👋

I'm Milind, a Senior Full-Stack Engineer based in Pune, India, with **7+ years** of experience shipping production software across **FinTech, e-commerce, and enterprise** domains. At [KraftPixel Digital Solutions](https://kraftpixel.com) I lead a team of four developers and own architecture, sprint delivery, and production reliability for business-critical platforms.

Most of my day is Next.js, React, and TypeScript, with Node.js and PHP behind them, and Docker, Redis, Kubernetes, and Cloudflare underneath. I care about Core Web Vitals, clean architecture, and releases that don't wake anyone up at 2 AM.

Outside of work I write about engineering, over-engineer my own side projects, and still drop into a CS:GO lobby like it's 2016.
